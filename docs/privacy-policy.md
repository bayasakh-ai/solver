## The short version

Sudofolio reads a sudoku through your phone's camera and works it out on your phone.
It has no internet access. It does not send anything about you or your puzzles to the
developer or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

## What the app accesses

**Camera.** The app asks for the camera so it can find and read a sudoku puzzle. The
camera picture is analysed on your phone, in memory, while the scanner is open.

- No photo or video is saved to your phone's storage or gallery.
- When you check a reading, the app keeps one still picture of the puzzle in memory so
  you can compare it with what was read. It is discarded when you leave that screen or
  close the app.
- Camera pictures never leave your phone. The app does not have the permission it would
  need to use the internet.

You can use the app without the camera: choose *Not now* when it asks, and play the
built-in puzzles. You can allow or withdraw camera access at any time in Android's
settings (Settings > Apps > Sudofolio > Permissions).

**Your handwriting on a puzzle.** If the page you scan has digits you wrote, the app
reads them on your phone so it can carry them onto the digital board and check your
progress. They are treated like the rest of the picture: analysed on the phone, never
sent anywhere.

## What the app stores on your phone

- **Your settings:** the answer colour, the game-board options, whether the welcome
  pages have been shown, and the camera options.
- **Your game in progress:** the puzzle's digits, the digits and notes you entered, the
  time played, hints used, mistakes counted and recent undo steps, so you can continue
  after closing the app. A finished game is removed.

This is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It contains no name, email
address, location, contacts or device identifiers. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You
can also delete it at any time in Android's settings (Settings > Apps > Sudofolio >
Storage > Clear storage).

## Backups made by your phone

If you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the
settings and game described above in the backup of your phone to your own Google
account, or copy them to a new phone when you set one up. That is done by Android, not
by the app, and the developer cannot see it. You control it in your phone's backup
settings.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.
- It does not use advertising or analytics services, and it does not use an
  advertising ID.
- It does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, files or photos.
- It does not require an account or sign-in.

## Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle tool and is not directed at children. It collects
no personal data from anyone, children included.

## Third-party software

The app includes open-source software that runs on your phone (camera, image
processing and a small digit-reading model). As built into this app, none of it sends
data off your phone.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if a future version
adds internet access, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and
the date above will change. A future version may offer optional features that need the
internet; they would be off unless you turn them on, and described here first.
