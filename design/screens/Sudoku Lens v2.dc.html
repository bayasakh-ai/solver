Ink blue, in two steps
Answers use one hue at two lightness steps. The app checks what’s behind each cell. On paper it draws deep blue #1D4FB0 with a thin white halo. On a dark screen it draws bright blue #8CC8FF with a thin dark halo. Blue looks like pen ink, stays clear of black print and red pencil marks, and is still distinct for people with red–green color blindness.
